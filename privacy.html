Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

PixelPlay Arcade is designed to let you play without creating an account. Game nickname preferences, round counts and scores are stored locally in your browser using local storage. They are not currently sent to a PixelPlay server. Clearing your browser storage may erase them.
