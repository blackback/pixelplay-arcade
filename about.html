About PixelPlay Arcade

PixelPlay Arcade is a small collection of original, quick-to-play browser games. The idea is simple: open a page, choose a challenge and enjoy a few minutes away from the scroll.

What you can play

Test your reaction speed, match hidden pairs, guide a neon snake, tap moving targets or combine number tiles. The games are designed to work on modern desktop and mobile browsers without downloads.

Scores and privacy

You can play and submit scores without registering or providing an email. Your nickname and personal stats are stored in your browser, while submitted nicknames and scores appear on the public global leaderboard. Only the highest score for each nickname per game is shown. Basic server-side score limits and rate limits help reduce abuse, but browser games cannot guarantee that every score is cheat-proof.
