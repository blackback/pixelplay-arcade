Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

PixelPlay Arcade does not require an account, email address or password. Your chosen nickname, round counts and personal scores are stored locally in your browser. When you submit a score, your public nickname, game and score are sent to our leaderboard database and may be displayed publicly. Clearing browser storage may erase your local preferences and personal stats, but does not remove scores already submitted to the public leaderboard.

Information and analytics

We do not ask for registration details or collect email addresses for gameplay. Our score-submission service processes an IP address to apply abuse-prevention rate limits; a one-way hash is used for rate limiting rather than publishing the address. Our hosting and database providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to deliver, secure and operate the service. If analytics or additional services are added, this policy should be updated before they are enabled.

Advertising and cookies

Advertising is not currently enabled on this site. If third-party advertising, such as Google AdSense, is added in the future, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalize ads, subject to their own policies and applicable law. This policy will be updated and any required consent mechanism will be implemented before advertising is activated.

Children

The games are general-audience entertainment and are not designed to collect information from children. Parents or guardians who have questions can contact the site operator.
